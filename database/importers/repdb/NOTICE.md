`8350dcaea79cd1013a140887ae4f0a3369bc4b73`.

Licensed under the **MIT License** (package code and the `minimal` style assets specifically —

different, mandatory visible credit to its illustrator). No visible in-app attribution is legally
required for the `minimal` assets used here; this notice documents the source and version for
